Double-entry rules

AssetsDebit ↑ · Credit ↓
Expenses & drawingsDebit ↑ · Credit ↓
LiabilitiesCredit ↑ · Debit ↓
Capital & revenueCredit ↑ · Debit ↓

Capital items change the balance sheet only. Revenue items flow through the income statement into profit. Every transaction has equal debit and credit.
